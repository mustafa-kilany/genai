# Images Directory

## House of Court Jordan Images

Please replace the placeholder images in `about.html` with actual photographs of the House of Court in Jordan.

### Recommended Sources:

1. **Official Sources:**
   - Jordanian Judicial Authority website
   - House of Court official website
   - Ministry of Justice

2. **Public Domain/Licensed Sources:**
   - Wikimedia Commons (search for "House of Court Jordan" or "مجلس القضاء الأردني")
   - Licensed stock photo services
   - Official government photo archives

### Image Requirements:

- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Recommended Size:** 1920x1080 or larger
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 4:3
- **File Names:**
  - `house-of-court-exterior.jpg`
  - `house-of-court-interior.jpg`
  - `house-of-court-architecture.jpg`

### How to Replace:

1. Download or obtain the images
2. Save them in this `public/images/` directory
3. Update the image `src` attributes in `about.html`:
   ```html
   <img src="/images/house-of-court-exterior.jpg" alt="House of Court Jordan - Exterior">
   ```

The current implementation uses Unsplash placeholder images. These should be replaced with actual House of Court images.
