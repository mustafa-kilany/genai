About Our Legal Assistant

Empowering citizens with accessible legal information

Overview

The Jordanian Legal Assistant is an AI-powered platform designed to help citizens, legal professionals, and businesses navigate Jordanian laws and regulations. Our system provides instant access to legal information, case law, and regulatory guidance.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To democratize access to legal information in Jordan by providing an intelligent, user-friendly platform that makes legal knowledge accessible to everyone.

Vision

To become the leading digital legal assistant in Jordan, helping citizens understand their rights and obligations while promoting legal literacy across the nation.

Key Features

  • AI-powered legal question answering with citations
  • Search functionality for laws, articles, and regulations
  • Case law database and precedent search
  • Fine and penalty calculation tools
  • Multi-turn conversation support for complex queries
  • Real-time legal updates and notifications

Technology

Built with cutting-edge AI technology powered by Google Gemini, our system combines natural language processing with a comprehensive legal knowledge base to provide accurate and helpful responses.

  • Backend: Express.js with Node.js
  • Database: MongoDB for scalable data storage
  • AI Engine: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash
  • Frontend: Bootstrap 5 with modern responsive design

Ethics & Responsibility

We are committed to ethical AI practices and responsible use of technology. Our system is designed to provide information, not legal advice. Users should consult qualified legal professionals for specific legal matters.

  • Transparent AI decision-making processes
  • Privacy-first data handling
  • Regular system audits and improvements
  • User feedback integration
