How It Works

Understanding our AI-powered legal assistant system

Step-by-Step Usage

1

Ask Your Question

Type your legal question in natural language. You can ask about laws, regulations, cases, or request calculations.

2

AI Processing

Our AI analyzes your question, searches the legal database, and determines if any tools are needed (search, calculation, etc.).

3

Get Your Answer

Receive a comprehensive answer with citations, relevant laws, and any calculations performed. You can ask follow-up questions for clarification.

4

Rate & Improve

Rate the response to help us improve. Your feedback helps train the system to provide better answers.

Technical Process

When you submit a question, the system follows this process:

  • Input Processing: Your question is received and validated
  • Context Retrieval: System checks conversation history for context
  • AI Analysis: Google Gemini AI analyzes the question
  • Tool Selection: AI decides which tools (if any) to use
  • Data Retrieval: Tools search laws, cases, or perform calculations
  • Response Generation: AI synthesizes information into a clear answer
  • Logging: Query and response are logged for evaluation

Available Tools

The AI assistant has access to several specialized tools:

  • Search Laws: Search through Jordanian legal database
  • Calculate: Perform legal calculations (fines, penalties, etc.)
  • Read File: Access specific legal documents
  • Search Cases: Find relevant legal cases and precedents

Example Questions

Here are some examples of questions you can ask:

General Legal Questions:
"What is the Personal Data Protection Law in Jordan?"
"Explain Article 5 of the PDPL"
Calculations:
"Calculate the fine for a data breach under PDPL"
"What is 5% of 10,000 JOD?"
Case Law:
"Find cases related to data privacy violations"
"Show me precedents for cybercrime cases"

Tips for Best Results

  • Be specific in your questions
  • Ask follow-up questions for clarification
  • Use natural language - no need for legal jargon
  • Rate responses to help improve the system
  • Check citations for verification
